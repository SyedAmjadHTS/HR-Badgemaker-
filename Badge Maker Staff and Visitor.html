ID Badge Printer

New Bridge Group – staff ID card
Drag the photo on the card to line up the face. Mouse wheel also zooms.
In the print dialog choose the Fargo printer, paper size CR80 (85.6×54 mm), margins None, scale 100%, and turn Headers and footers off.
The back of the card is the same for everyone. Load a card with the blank side set to print, then click Print back.
Same print settings as the front: CR80 (85.6×54 mm), margins None, scale 100%, headers and footers off.
